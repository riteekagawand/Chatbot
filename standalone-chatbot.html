My Website with Contentstack ChatBot

This is a demo of how to integrate the Contentstack ChatBot into any website. Click the chat button in the bottom right to start chatting!

Features:

  • ✅ Fetches real data from Contentstack CMS
  • ✅ Answers accommodation questions
  • ✅ Handles booking, payment, cancellation queries
  • ✅ Shows tour information
  • ✅ Works with your existing Contentstack setup

Try asking:

  • "What type of accommodation do you provide?"
  • "How many tours do you have?"
  • "How can I book a tour?"
  • "What's your cancellation policy?"
